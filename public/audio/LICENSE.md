# Original coin sound effects

income.wav, expense.wav, transfer.wav were generated specifically for this project
using the deterministic physical impact model in scripts/generate_coin_audio.py.
No third-party recordings, samples, APIs, or musical compositions were used.

These assets and their generation script are dedicated to the public domain under
CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Income: three light metallic impacts. Expense: lower, shorter coin movement.
Transfer: two separated coin movements. Recommended defaults are bundled here;
perceived realism and pleasantness still require owner listening and device QA.
